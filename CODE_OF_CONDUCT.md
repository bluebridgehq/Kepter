# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kepter to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, background, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new
- Respect different views and experiences
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Pressuring maintainers or other contributors for assignments, reviews or rewards
- Any other conduct that would be out of place in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and act on behaviour in any project space.

| Level | When | What happens |
|:------|:-----|:-------------|
| **Warning** | A first or minor breach | A private message explaining what was wrong |
| **Temporary ban** | Repeated or more serious breaches | No interaction with the project for a set time |
| **Permanent ban** | Severe breaches, or continuing after a temporary ban | No further part in the project |

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to a maintainer through GitHub, or by email to the address on the [Blue Bridge](https://github.com/bluebridgehq) profile. Every report is reviewed, and the person who reported it is kept confidential.
